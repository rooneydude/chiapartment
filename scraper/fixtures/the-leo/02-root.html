Life at The Leo

The Leo boasts 21 stories of open-concept living spaces in the heart of River North. Designed by Antunovich Associates, these residences are meticulously crafted to harmonize work and leisure. Revel in kitchens tailored for culinary mastery and take-out ease, embrace streamlined routines with in-unit washer/dryers, bask in the sun on the rooftop pool deck, and utilize the co-working lounge crafted for the ambitious hustler in you.
