Pet Policy

Pet Policy Type: Cat, Dogs
Max Number of Pets: 2
Max weight of pets: 100
Pet monthly rent min/max: $25 - $35
Pet policy fee min/max: $250 - $500

Pet policy: We welcome 2 pets per apartment home. There is a one-time pet fee of $500.00 per dog and $250.00 per cat. The monthly pet rent is $35.00 per dog and $25.00 per cat. There is a combined weight limit of 100lbs per apartment and aggressive breeds are prohibited. Please contact our leasing office for more information about our Pet Policy.

Welcome Home

Nestled in the heart of Old Town, 1225 blends historic charm with modern luxury. Surrounded by Victorian-era architecture, tree-lined streets, and vibrant destinations, this boutique-style community offers an unmatched living experience.

Inside, each residence features hardwood-style flooring, panoramic city views, and designer kitchens with steel-finished appliances and quartz countertops. Residents enjoy premium amenities, including a 24-hour fitness center, outdoor heated pool, and private conference room, along with top-tier services like package receiving and Tide laundry.

With 16 stories and 30,000+ square feet of retail space, 1225 Old Town isn’t just a place to live—it’s a destination. Discover boutique rental living at its finest. Schedule your tour today.
